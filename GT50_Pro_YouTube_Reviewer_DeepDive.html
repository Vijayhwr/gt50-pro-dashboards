    What's missing from the mix

    • Premium reviewers whose audiences buy ₹50–70K phones (Tech Burner, TechBar). Today's line-up anchors on ₹25–35K.
    • BGMI esports names (Jonathan, Scout, LoLzZz) who can make "120 FPS for an hour" believable against iQOO and POCO.
    • Tamil and Malayalam long-form reviews. Telugu (Prasad) was the best regional performer.
    • A plan for big creators who aren't seeded: Manoj Saru, the biggest channel not seeded, filled the gap with a negative take.

    What worked

    • Trakin's day-one unboxing was the single biggest video, and Prasad brought the south in at the same scale.
    • Shorts built around liquid cooling (Techno Ruhez, Techy Pathshala) beat those channels' usual reach.
    • Venom's 25-minute review drew the most balanced comments and genuine creator trust.
    • RSGT Plays is an organic GT-series superfan with reach across five videos. He needs the right price facts.

    Controlling the narrative on YouTube

    Platform-level moves for the next 14 days

    Correct the price record at source

    • Ask TecHype, Corona Tech, RSGT Plays, Tech Merchant, Sai Tech Guru and Gobind Gore to update titles and descriptions, and to pin a correction stating the official price: ₹69,999, including a ₹24,999 kit; ₹64,999 after the bank offer.
    • Post a 45-second official Short, "Why ₹69,999: what's in the box", and reply with it under every price-speculation video.
    • Stop price-guess teasers before launch: the brand's "Guess the Price!" invited low anchors.

    — views carried a lower price

    Own the top comment on seeded videos

    • Agree with seeded creators that they pin a value-breakdown comment within the first hour, before a price joke takes the top spot. Trakin's top comment is a price joke.
    • Have the creator heart and reply to two or three genuine owner comments so they rise.
    • Add a chapter marker such as "₹24,999 accessory kit explained" so the context shows in search and on the timeline.

    — comments are about price

    Bring in the critic, not just the fans

    • Seed Manoj Saru now with a full kit and a 7-day test brief. He is the biggest channel that isn't seeded, and his audience already distrusts the price.
    • Brief creators to address "why not POCO X7 Pro / iQOO 15R" on camera with sustained-FPS charts.
    • Drop identical briefed titles. Four Shorts read "First Time In India – Gaming Ecosystem", and viewers call them ads.

    — views on the critic's video

    Lead with the hook that's working

    • Liquid cooling is the thumb-stopper. Make a Shorts series of one-hour BGMI 120 FPS runs with live temperatures, tested against iQOO 15R and POCO X8 Pro.
    • Answer requests for cooler tests: "GT MagCharge cooler 2.0 testing karke dikhao".
    • Run a KRAFTON / BGMI esports moment to certify 120 FPS with a third party.

    — views on the cooling Short

    Address the phone-only ask openly

    • The most consistent constructive ask is a phone-only version at ₹35–45K. Either confirm a roadmap or explain the kit value in every reply.
    • Use a YouTube Community post to show the kit's itemised value and take questions.

    — comments ask for the phone alone

    Protect trust with existing GT owners

    • GT 20/30 Pro owners use launch videos to chase updates. Post a pinned update timeline on the brand channel and send a service team to reply on high-traffic videos.
    • Set a 24-hour reply rule for update and warranty comments on the top 10 videos.
