Decision needed

A phone-only SKU and a value-led relaunch of the ecosystem story, before the price narrative sets in.

    For the CXO table

    Five decisions this data supports

    Launch a phone-only SKU

    The most consistent ask: "launch the standalone version", "34,999 ✅ 69,999 ❌". A ₹35–40K phone-only price brings back the buyers the bundle priced out, without diluting the kit.

    — comments ask for the phone without the kit

    Sell the bundle as value, not as a price

    Creator posts that walk through controller, dock and cooler "in one box" did best. Lead with the itemised value of the kit and how much a buyer saves, and give creators that framing.

    — plays on the top "worth it?" creator reel

    Win the spec-sheet fight on gameplay

    Rival-chip comparisons (iQOO 15R, OPPO K13 Turbo Pro, POCO X8 Pro) dominate YouTube. Answer with sustained-FPS and thermal proof from long gaming sessions, not with spec claims.

    — comments name a competitor

    Run a service-recovery sprint for GT 20/30 owners

    Owners of older GT phones are using launch posts to raise update and warranty problems, and some are spamming "don't buy" on brand posts. Set up a public-reply protocol and a fast-track service desk now.

    — comments about service or older-device updates

    Retire #GT50 and consolidate on owned tags

    #GT50 is shared with a viral ₹5K camera drone, so our posts get lost among drone reviews. Move all amplification to #InfinixGT50Pro and #GameTogether.
